None （57）摘要
本发明公开了一种环磺酮的制备方法，属于有机化工技术领域，该方法采用三氟乙醇钠与2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺基苯甲酸甲酯反应，生成的2-氯-3-三氟乙氧甲基-4-甲磺基苯甲酸与1，3-环己二酮缩合重排制备环磺酮。本发明提供的环磺酮的制备方法，克服了传统方法中用2-氯-3-溴甲基-4-甲磺基苯甲酸甲酯和三氟乙醇、叔丁醇钾制备2-氯-3-三氟乙氧甲基-4-甲磺基苯甲酸收率低，叔丁醇钾试剂贵的缺点，同时也克服了羧基酰氯化成酯后重排法中需要剧毒催化剂丙酮氰醇的缺点，具有收率高、成本低、易操作、污染少、安全环保等优点，适合大规模工业化生产。
    > EN: (57) Abstract
The present invention discloses a process for the preparation of tembotrione, belonging to the technical field of organic chemical industry. The process uses sodium trifluoroethoxide to react with methyl 2-chloro-3-bromomethyl-4-methanesulfonylbenzoate, and the resulting 2-chloro-3-trifluoroethoxymethyl-4-methanesulfonylbenzoic acid is condensed and rearranged with 1,3-cyclohexanedione to prepare tembotrione. The preparation process for tembotrione provided by the present invention overcomes the shortcomings of the traditional process, in which methyl 2-chloro-3-bromomethyl-4-methanesulfonylbenzoate is used with trifluoroethanol and potassium tert-butoxide to prepare 2-chloro-3-trifluoroethoxymethyl-4-methanesulfonylbenzoic acid in low yield and the potassium tert-butoxide reagent is expensive; at the same time it overcomes the shortcoming of the route in which the carboxyl group is converted to the acid chloride and then to the ester followed by rearrangement, which requires the highly toxic catalyst acetone cyanohydrin. It has the advantages of high yield, low cost, ease of operation, little pollution and safety for the environment, and is suitable for large-scale industrial production.

    > EN: The present invention relates to the field of organic chemical technology, and specifically to a process for preparing tembotrione.
[0002] 环磺酮是迄今为止活性较高、用量较少，且比较安全的一类除草剂，其工业制备方法主要是三氟乙醇、叔丁醇钾法制备关键中间体2-氯-3-三氟乙氧甲基-4-甲磺基苯甲酸然后羧基酰氯化成酯后重排法，且该种方法获得成品的收率较低，用到的试剂昂贵。其中，在制备关键中间体2-氯-3-三氟乙氧甲基-4-甲磺基苯甲酸采用叔丁醇钾做碱，收率较低、试剂昂贵；羧基酰氯化成酯后重排法要用到剧毒试剂丙酮氰醇为重排催化剂，且需要用二氯亚砜作酰氯化试剂，而二氯亚砜对环境有很大危害。

    > EN: In summary, the traditional methods of preparing tembotrione all have the characteristics of low yield, high cost and the presence of highly toxic substances in the production process, and do not meet the requirements of large-scale industrial production.
